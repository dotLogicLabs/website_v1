Our Process

From threat model to hardened deployment.

01

Threat Modeling & Assessment

We map the attack surface of the specific hardware, network, and data flows involved — not a generic checklist.

02

Architecture & Controls Design

Security controls designed into the system architecture, sized to the actual risk rather than added as a blanket policy.

03

Where This Applies

Wherever devices and data need protecting.

  • IoT device fleets — authentication and network segmentation across large numbers of connected devices.
  • Industrial control systems — securing operational technology without disrupting uptime requirements.
  • Regulated environments — architectures designed with the compliance and audit trail requirements built in from the start.
  • Startups building connected products — security designed in at the architecture stage, before it's expensive to retrofit.
