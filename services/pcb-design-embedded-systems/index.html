Our Process

From concept to production-ready board.

01

Requirements & Architecture

We scope the electrical, mechanical, and firmware requirements against your actual deployment environment — power budget, enclosure, certification targets.

02

Schematic & Layout

Component selection and PCB layout with manufacturability, EMI, and thermal behavior considered from the first revision.

03

Prototype & Bring-Up

Assembled boards go through firmware bring-up and functional testing before any design is treated as final.

04

Production Handoff

DFM-reviewed files, test procedures, and documentation handed to your contract manufacturer or ours — with support through first production run.

FAQ

Common questions on hardware engagements.

Do you work with early-stage prototypes or only production-ready designs?

Both. Many engagements start at a rough breadboard or even a concept sketch — we scope the path to production from wherever you're starting.

Can you take an existing board and optimize it for cost or power?

Yes. Redesigns for cost-down, power optimization, or component obsolescence are common — we audit the existing design before proposing changes.

Do you handle firmware as well as the board itself?

Yes — firmware engineering is part of this competency, not a separate handoff, so hardware and firmware decisions get made together.
