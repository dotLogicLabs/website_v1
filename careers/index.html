FAQ

Common questions about roles here.

Why is PCB & embedded systems on-site while everything else is remote?

Hardware bring-up, lab equipment, and physical prototypes are hard to do justice remotely — this role works directly with the boards and test equipment on-site.

What does the MTS level mean here?

Member of Technical Staff is our single level across all open roles right now — a hands-on engineering role with real ownership over what you build, not a junior or bounded scope position.

Do you hire outside standard business hours or time zones for remote roles?

Yes — remote roles are evaluated on output and overlap for collaboration, not a fixed office time zone.
